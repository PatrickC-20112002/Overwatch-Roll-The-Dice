# 🎲 Roll The Dice

**Roll The Dice (RTD)** is a custom **Overwatch Workshop Deathmatch / Free-For-All** mode inspired by the Roll the Dice plugin from *Team Fortress 2*.

Press **Interact** to roll one of **157 random perks** that can help you, hurt you, or completely change how you play. Perks range from simple buffs and debuffs to unique abilities, hero transformations, and challenges.

## How to Play

**Current Version:** V2.5  
**Workshop Code:** `4C92C`

Import the code into an Overwatch Custom Game and start a Deathmatch.

Press **Interact** to roll the dice. Illari, Lifeweaver, Symmetra and Torbjörn can also roll with **Crouch**. Each perk lasts for a limited time or until its conditions are met. Once it ends, you'll have a cooldown before you can roll again. **Getting Final Blows reduces your dice cooldown**, allowing you to roll more frequently.

Some perks also have their own controls, abilities, or objectives, which are explained in the perk list below.

## Favourite Perks

Before entering the match, you can choose a **Favourite Perk** to increase your chances of rolling it.

- **Normal Favourites** have a default **1 in 5 chance** of being rolled.
- **Rare Favourites** have a **1 in 10 chance** and are highlighted in orange.
- If your favourite isn't rolled, you'll receive a random perk as usual.
- To change your favourite, press **Interact** while dead or in a spawn room, once your dice is ready. Choose **No Favourite** for completely random rolls.

Use **Primary/Secondary Fire** to browse the Favourite Perk menu, **Abilities 1/2** to skip through perks, and **Interact** to confirm your selection.

Favourite Perk chances can be adjusted in the Workshop settings, and the system can also be disabled.
